# Items for a Ugandan senior to confirm

The study notes were researched and then independently fact-checked. However, the research
environment could **not open the Uganda Clinical Guidelines 2023 (UCG), the Uganda HIV
Consolidated Guidelines 2022, or WHO/NCBI pages directly**. Doses were checked against
guideline knowledge and search summaries. None of the items below is known to be wrong. They
are places where Ugandan protocol may differ, or where the exact figure could not be confirmed.
Please confirm them with a registrar or consultant, or against the current UCG/MoH documents,
and then update the notes.

## Drugs and regimens

- **Hydralazine**: maximum "20 mg IV per episode". Check whether UCG says 20 mg per 24 h.
- **Antihypertensive start threshold** in gestational hypertension: the notes now use 140/90 (NICE/CHAP). Check the local protocol.
- **Nifedipine tocolysis**: the local maximum daily dose (many protocols cap at 160 mg/day).
- **Vaginal discharge / STI syndromic regimen and PID** (UCG 2023): ceftriaxone dose (1 g vs 250–500 mg) and route; cefixime alternative.
- **Septic abortion, puerperal sepsis and obstructed labour antibiotics**: the WHO MCPC triple regimen is given, with ceftriaxone + metronidazole as the common local alternative. Confirm the UCG first line.
- **Caesarean prophylaxis**: ceftriaxone ± metronidazole (Uganda practice) vs cefazolin 2 g (international).
- **Oxytocin infusion concentrations** (PPH 20 IU/1 L; augmentation 2.5 vs 5 IU/500 mL): check the unit protocol.
- **Anti-D**: UK doses and the 1,500 IU vial usually stocked in Uganda are both shown. Check local availability and policy.
- **Uncomplicated malaria in the first trimester**: artemether–lumefantrine (WHO 2022) vs quinine (older UCG).
- **Sickle cell disease malaria prevention**: daily proguanil vs IPTp-SP with folic acid 5 mg withheld.
- **Anaemia in pregnancy**: ferrous sulphate 200 mg twice vs three times daily; transfusion thresholds.
- **Neonatal hypoglycaemia**: treatment threshold (2.6 mmol/L locally vs NICE 2.0).

## HIV and hepatitis B (Uganda Consolidated Guidelines 2022)

- Viral load schedule in pregnancy and breastfeeding, and the "non-suppressed" cut-off (1,000 vs 200 copies/mL).
- High-risk infant prophylaxis (AZT + NVP durations, extended NVP), birth PCR, and co-trimoxazole for all pregnant women with HIV.
- HBV: tenofovir start (28 vs 28–32 weeks) and whether all HBsAg-positive women now qualify.

## Screening and programmes

- National cervical screening intervals (VIA → HPV-based transition), the single-dose HPV vaccine start date (2025), and the vaccine product.
- The WHO age to stop cervical screening for women living with HIV.
- Uganda waste colour coding (green vs red bins) and chlorine concentration for high-level disinfection (0.1% vs 0.5%).

## Monitoring and definitions

- Labour Care Guide maternal pulse frequency (hourly vs 4-hourly).
- The Ugandan definition of APH (from 24 or 28 weeks).
- Ventouse abandon rule (2 vs 3 detachments; 15–20 vs 30 min).
- DMPA in breastfeeding women before 6 weeks postpartum (WHO MEC category 3): check the current Uganda FP guidance.

## Epidemiology figures (approximate; from search summaries)

UDHS 2022 (MMR 189/100,000, NMR 22/1,000, ANC4+ ~72%, CS rate ~7–8%), Uganda fistula burden, preterm
birth rate, sickle trait prevalence, cervical cancer incidence (GLOBOCAN 2022), and E-MOTIVE trial size.

## Obs & Gyn Pharmacology (drug handbook)

- Restarting MgSO₄ after toxicity (0.5 g/h, or delay/halve the IM dose): confirm the unit protocol.
- Dexamethasone ampoule strength in local stock (handbook assumes 4 mg/mL, so 6 mg = 1.5 mL).
- Acute tocolysis for oxytocin hyperstimulation: terbutaline 0.25 mg SC vs salbutamol infusion (10 mg in 1 L at 10 drops/min, WHO MCPC); which does the unit stock?
- Tranexamic acid for heavy menstrual bleeding: 1 g orally 8-hourly for up to 4–5 days.
- Whether UCG 2023 has adopted artemether-lumefantrine for first-trimester malaria (WHO 2022) or still prefers quinine + clindamycin.
- STI doses in pregnancy (ceftriaxone 500 mg IM or cefixime 400 mg + azithromycin 1 g) and PID ceftriaxone dose against the UCG 2023 syndromic packages.
- Penicillin allergy in syphilis (ceftriaxone course vs desensitisation) and parenteral iron doses (iron sucrose 200 mg; ferric carboxymaltose up to 1,000 mg / 20 mg/kg).
- Lipid emulsion regimen for LAST (1.5 mL/kg, then 15 mL/kg/h, max 12 mL/kg) and whether it is stocked.
- LNG emergency contraception 3 mg for BMI >26 or weight >70 kg (FSRH advice; WHO does not specify).
- PTU/carbimazole and domperidone doses; atosiban regimen (rarely available).

## Paediatrics: neonatology (week 1)

- Neonatal tetanus: metronidazole course length (UCG 2023 reads 14 days; many references 7–10); equine antitoxin 5,000 IU (older WHO protocols; UCG gives TIG 500 IU only).
- Neonatal ampicillin frequency: UCG hospital section 6-hourly vs WHO/IMNCI 12-hourly in week 1 then 8-hourly. Gentamicin in LBW: WHO 3 mg/kg vs UCG 4 mg/kg (preterm).
- Uganda facts: MNT elimination validated 2011; Nsambya human milk bank 2021; which hospitals have bubble CPAP, exchange transfusion, ROP screening, Ponseti clinics; G6PD deficiency common / Rh-negative uncommon.
- Numbers: HIE prognosis (≈20–40% death/disability in moderate HIE; Thompson score cut-off ≈15); feeding methods by weight band; Ballard/reflex disappearance ages; NICE phototherapy values beyond 60 h (extrapolated) and the "GA × 10 − 100" preterm threshold rule. AAP 2022 thresholds not quoted (page not readable).

## Paediatrics: HIV, immunisation, social paediatrics

- pDTG at 3–5.9 kg: UCG table prints 1 tab; FDA/WHO dose is 5 mg (½ × 10 mg dispersible). Neonatal (<4 weeks) first-line regimen; 3HP eligibility in children.
- High-risk infant prophylaxis: UCG 2023 says NVP alone × 12 weeks; site follows hiv-in-pregnancy (AZT+NVP 6 weeks then NVP). PEP: UCG lists LPV/r or ATV/r; notes prefer DTG-based.
- Immunisation: rotavirus doses (MoH poster 6/10/14 wk vs UCG 2023 6/10 wk); IPV timing (UCG 2023: 6 and 14 weeks).
- Statistics: UDHS 2022 (IMR 36, U5MR 52, stunting 24%, perinatal mortality); VACS 2015 figures; child marriage/teen pregnancy; paediatric HIV numbers.
- Law: Children (Amendment) Act 2016 section numbers (corporal punishment ban, duty to report); adolescent consent age for HIV testing/SRH; UNCST assent age (8 years).
- Doses: paediatric STI prophylaxis after defilement (ceftriaxone 25–50 mg/kg max 250 mg; metronidazole); rectal artesunate capsule strength in iCCM (50 vs 100 mg).

## Paediatrics: emergencies, hypoglycaemia, poisoning, endocrine, skin

- Neonatal hypoglycaemia threshold: site uses 2.6 mmol/L (WHO Pocket Book 2.2 mmol/L).
- Shock fluids: WHO ETAT 2016 10–20 mL/kg over 30–60 min vs UCG 2023 20–30 mL/kg over 60 min (up to 60 mL/kg). Which does Mengo use?
- Phenobarbital loading: WHO 15 mg/kg (neonates 20), UCG 10–15 mg/kg in severe malaria, 20 mg/kg common.
- Organophosphate atropine: WHO 0.02 mg/kg vs UCG 0.05 mg/kg doubling every 3–5 min.
- Tinea capitis griseofulvin: UCG 10 mg/kg/day × 6 weeks vs international 15–25 mg/kg/day. Benzyl benzoate repeat schedule. Ivermectin limits (UCG <15 kg or <12 years; WHO ≥15 kg).
- Zinc for diarrhoea: WHO 2024 suggests 5 mg/day; UCG still 10/20 mg.
- Infant CPR: confirm AHA 2025 dropped the two-finger technique. Snakebite: antivenom per insert; neostigmine dose per local protocol.
- DKA without pump: UCG wording for IM/SC insulin in children (notes: 0.1 unit/kg hourly); ISPAD add-dextrose point 14–17 mmol/L. Paracetamol: only the 21-hour NAC regimen taught.
- Uganda figures: U5MR 52, stunting ~24% (UDHS 2022); insulin via Changing Diabetes in Children / Life for a Child.

## Paediatrics: respiratory and TB

- Asthma: UCG 2023 lists no IV MgSO₄ for children and prednisolone 1 mg/kg; notes say Kampala referral wards give MgSO₄ 40–50 mg/kg (max 2 g) before aminophylline. Confirm GINA 2025 steps for 6–11 years.
- Bronchiolitis: UCG allows salbutamol trial, nebulised adrenaline and 150 mL/kg/day fluids (conflicts with NICE/WHO); notes advise supportive care. Croup fluid (half-strength Darrow's).
- Otitis media amoxicillin (UCG 15 mg/kg 8-hourly vs WHO/IMCI 40 mg/kg 12-hourly); benzathine penicillin <30 kg (30,000 IU/kg vs 600,000 IU); diphtheria antitoxin doses and WHO 2024 macrolide preference; UCG erythromycin "50 mg/kg 6-hourly" read as a typo (12.5 mg/kg).
- TB: use of the 4-month regimen in Uganda (and in SAM/advanced HIV); TPT for MDR-TB contacts (UCG none vs WHO 2024 levofloxacin); pyridoxine in infants; 3HP weight bands; Keith Edwards score items; WHO algorithm cut-off (>10); stool Xpert method. TB/HIV: UCG table still EFV/NVP; notes follow DTG twice daily with rifampicin. BCG timing for babies of mothers with infectious TB.

## Paediatrics: blood, cancers, cardiac, renal

- Sickle cell: hydroxyurea rule at Mulago (all SCA from 9 months vs UCG indications); malaria prevention (monthly SP vs DHA-piperaquine). TRACT 30 mL/kg transfusion and post-discharge malaria chemoprevention: adopted locally?
- Malaria hyperparasitaemia transfusion threshold: UCG >20% vs WHO Pocket Book >10% (both given).
- Nephrotic syndrome: local steroid regimen (6+6 weeks or UCG taper vs IPNA/KDIGO 4+4 weeks); local steroid-resistance rate (~20–40% quoted). Kidney failure fluids: UCG 10 mL/kg/day + losses vs ~400 mL/m²/day insensible.
- Rheumatic fever: benzathine penicillin weight cut-off (UCG 30 kg vs AHA 27 kg); UCG prophylaxis durations as quoted; latent RHD prevalence (~1.5–3%); regional RHD clinics (Lira, Gulu, Mbarara) and regional cancer centres; WHF 2023 echo staging summarised only.
- Mulago paediatric dialysis capacity; where prostaglandin E1 is available in Uganda.
